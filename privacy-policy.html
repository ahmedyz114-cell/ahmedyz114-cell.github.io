This Privacy Policy explains how Super Nino's World handles information when you play the mobile app or web version.

1. Game Progress & Local Storage

Super Nino's World works offline-first. Your unlocked stages, Star Coins, equipped hero skins, boosters, daily reward streaks, mission progress, and audio/haptic preferences are saved strictly locally on your device via browser/WebView localStorage. We do not operate external accounts or upload your gameplay data to our own servers.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob) & Consent

To keep Super Nino's World free to play, mobile builds may display advertisements (banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video ads) served by Google AdMob.

  • Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, coarse location, app interaction events, and diagnostic metrics to deliver, cap, and measure advertisements.
  • In regions where consent is required (including the EEA and UK), the app supports Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent prompt so you can choose personalized or non-personalized advertising preferences.
  • You can reset or limit ad personalization anytime on Android via Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google Settings).
  • Review Google's partner policy here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information (such as names, emails, or phone numbers) from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). If the app is configured for a mixed or child audience in Google Play Console, ensure AdMob child-directed treatment settings are enabled to match Google Play Families policies.

4. Deleting Your Data

You can delete all local game data immediately inside the game via Pause → Reset Saved Progress, or by clearing app storage / uninstalling the app on Android.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
